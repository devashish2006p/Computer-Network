# 1. Physical Layer 
Physical layer OSI Model ki sbse neeche wali layer hai joki data transmission ke actual physical medium se related hoti hai yani hardware level per communication kaise kaam karti hai. Ish layer ka kaam hai bits data ko electrical, optical or radio signals ma convert karke transmission karna physical medium ka through. 
# 2. Functions of Physical Layer
1. Bit Transmission – Data ko bits ke form me medium ke through bhejna. Ye decide karta hai kaun sa signal 1 represent krega aur konsa 0. 
2. Encoding/Decoding – Data bits ko transmission ka lia signals me convert (encode) karna, aur receiver par wapas signals se bits (decode) me convert karna. 
3. Modulation/ Demodulation – Analog communication me bits to carrier signal per modulate karna aur receiver me demodulate karna.
4. Bit Rate control – Transmission speed decide karna. 
5. Transmission Mode – Data kis direction me flow krega ye physical layer he decide karta hai. 
6. Physical Topology – Devices ka physical connection pattern define karna. Star, bus, ring, mesh etc. 
7. Transmission Medium – Kaunsa Medium use hoga : copper cable, Fiber optic ya wireless ya decide karna.  
8. Synchronization – Sender aur receiver ke timing ko match karna. 
9. Interface Standard – Devices ka connectors, pin numbers, voltage levels aur signal type define karna.
